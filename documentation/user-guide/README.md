# SG Finance Planner — User Guide

Plain-language guide to what the app does, how the pages fit together, and how to complete everyday tasks. For developers, see [Technical documentation](../README.md).

## Who this is for

Anyone using the app to plan money in Singapore: monthly budgets, cash and savings, credit cards, debt, CPF, investments, and optional extras (travel, poker). Figures are in **SGD** unless noted.

## How the app is organized

The **sidebar** groups pages into five areas:

| Area | What it’s for |
|------|----------------|
| **Overview** | See this month at a glance — calendar and cash in/out |
| **Planning** | Set targets: budget, savings goals, longer cashflow and wealth views, BTO |
| **Tracking** | Log spending, mark bills paid, review history, travel and poker |
| **Accounts** | Cash, cards, investments, CPF, and loans |
| **Configuration** | Your profile, salary, partner, and data backup |

You need to **sign in** (email magic link) for data to save in the cloud. Without sign-in, the app may run in a limited local-only mode.

## Quick answers

**Where do I log daily spending?**  
**Expenses** (by budget category) or **Transaction history** (everything in one list).

**How do credit cards know my spending?**  
When you add an expense, choose **Pay from** = your card. That spend counts toward the card’s current billing cycle on **Credit Cards**.

**What’s the difference between Budget and Expenses?**  
**Budget** sets how much you *plan* to spend per category. **Expenses** records what you *actually* spent each month.

**Can my partner see my data?**  
You can invite a partner on **ME** to share **household savings pools and goals**. Personal cash accounts and most tracking stay private to you.

**Where are dates and times?**  
The app uses **Singapore time** for “today” and when you record payments.
