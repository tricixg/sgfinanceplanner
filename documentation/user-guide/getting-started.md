# Getting started

## Signing in

1. Open the app and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Open the **magic link** in the email from Supabase (check spam if needed).
4. You land in the app, usually on **This Month**.

Your data is tied to your account and is private to you (protected in the database). Sign out from **ME** when using a shared computer.

## Finding your way around

- Use the **sidebar** on the left to switch pages.
- The **title and short description** at the top of each page explain what that screen is for.
- Many pages have **Edit** / **Save** for lists (cards, loans, budget lines) or **month arrows** to move between months.

## Ideas worth knowing

### Personal vs shared money

- **Cash accounts** and most tracking are **yours alone**.
- **Savings & Goals** can include **shared pools** if you link a partner — useful for joint savings, not for merging everyday expenses.

### Pay from (which account paid for something)

When you record spending, you can choose **Pay from**:

- A **cash** account (e.g. DBS, PayNow wallet) — updates that account’s balance in the app.
- A **credit card** — counts toward that card’s spending on the **Credit Cards** page; does not move cash until you pay the statement.

If you skip pay-from, the expense still counts toward your budget but won’t update cash or card tracking.

### Months

Expense and recurring screens work by **calendar month** (e.g. May 2026). Use **previous / next month** to review or backfill.

### Budget categories

On **Budget**, you define lines such as rent, groceries, or “Living & variable spend” with a monthly amount. On **Expenses**, you log individual purchases against those categories.

Some amounts on **Budget** come automatically from elsewhere:

- **Loans** and **insurance** you set under **Debts & Loans** and **ME**
- **Subscriptions** you define on **Recurring**

### Transaction history

**Transaction history** is one combined list of:

- Money in/out of cash accounts
- Expenses you entered
- Older imported card/budget rows (if any)

It’s the best place to search, edit, or delete a specific entry — not the only place you *create* spending (use **Expenses** or **Recurring** for that).

## Suggested first-time setup

1. **ME** — Enter salary and salary credit day; add insurance policies if you track them here.
2. **Cash Accounts** — Add your bank accounts and current balances.
3. **Credit Cards** — Add your cards (statement day and payment due day matter for cycles).
4. **Debts & Loans** — Add instalment plans or other loans.
5. **Budget** — Set category amounts for the month.
6. **Expenses** — Start logging spending with **Pay from** set correctly.

After that, use **This Month** and **This Month Cashflow** for a quick picture of the current month.
