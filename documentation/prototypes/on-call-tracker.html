Days count both the start and end date. Take-home is gross minus employee CPF. CPF is worked out per entry at the rates in settings, so it can differ by a few cents from your payslip. When you record a payment, the amount credited is what goes into income, so type in the payslip figure if it differs. In YTD, earned figures count days on call this year, and received figures count payments tagged to a salary month this year.
