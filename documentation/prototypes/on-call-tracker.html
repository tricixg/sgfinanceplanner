Days count both the start and end date. Take-home is gross minus employee CPF. CPF is worked out per entry at the rates in settings, so it can differ by a few cents from your payslip, where CPF is rounded on the whole month's pay. In YTD, an entry that crosses into this year only counts its days in this year.
